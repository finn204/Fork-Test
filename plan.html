Daily stretch · 5 minutes, evenings
Hip flexor stretch (half kneel)45s each
Doorway chest stretch45s
Thoracic extension over a chair45s
Hamstring stretch45s each
Cat-cow10 slow
Why these five. You sit at a desk all day and walk with a pack. That shortens hip flexors and chest, and rounds the upper back. These four open exactly those, and cat-cow keeps the spine moving. This is the posture work.
Gentle, never forced. With scoliosis, stretching should feel like a long easy pull and nothing else. No bouncing, no pushing into pain, no cranking your spine into a twist. If anything pinches, stop. A physio could tailor this properly if you ever want it done right.
Progression
The only rule that mattersHit the top of the rep range on every set, then add weight next session and drop back to the bottom of the range. Write down what you lifted. That is the whole system.
Turning up and picking machines is why nothing has moved. Same lifts, every week, slightly heavier. Boring works.

What not to do
Do not add anything to this plan for six weeks. No new tracker, no supplements, no second program, no reverse-engineering your watch. You already told me your risk is overcommitting and going manual. Four rules and one program is the whole thing. If you want to change something, change the weight on the bar.
